# In Your Language

The most important document for a student with epilepsy, the **seizure action plan**, is available
in several languages from established health organizations. We link those versions here rather
than translating medical information ourselves.

!!! note
    EDAN's own pages are in English for now. For the documents that matter most (the seizure action
    plan and the state toolkit), we point you to versions published by the Epilepsy Foundation of
    Minnesota and to samples the Minnesota Department of Health links from its toolkit. **In an emergency,
    call 911.**

## Official translated seizure materials
- **Epilepsy Foundation of Minnesota**, seizure action plan templates in English, Spanish, Hmong,
  and Somali: https://www.epilepsyfoundationmn.org/get-support/seizure-smart-trainings/
- **Minnesota Department of Health**, "Managing Seizures in School" toolkit, which links to sample
  plans from the Seizure Action Plan Coalition in English, Spanish, Simplified Chinese, Korean,
  Tagalog, and Vietnamese: https://www.health.state.mn.us/people/childrenyouth/schoolhealth/hco/seizurestoolkit.html
