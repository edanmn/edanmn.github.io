# 504, IEP or Health Plan: Path-Finder

Five questions about your child and your school day. A map fills in as you answer, so you can
see what you have established and where the decision is heading. At the end you get the document
that fits, who to send the request to, and a letter already filled in with your answers.

## The five questions

1. Does the school have a seizure diagnosis from your child's provider, in writing?
2. Is epilepsy affecting learning, or mainly safety and access?
3. Does the teaching itself need to change, or only the conditions around it?
4. Does your child need medication or a seizure response during the school day?
5. Is your child missing school?

Questions 2 and 3 decide which education plan the tool points you at. Question 4 decides what
Minnesota's seizure law owes you on top of that. Questions 1 and 5 shape the letter.

## What comes out

A Section 504 plan is the fit when learning is on track and what your child needs is the school
day arranged safely around epilepsy. An IEP is the fit when the teaching itself has to change,
which is special education under IDEA. When your answers point both ways, the tool tells you to
ask for both in one letter and let the evaluation sort it out. If you are not sure yet, it says
so and writes the evaluation request anyway.

Every result carries the same point about health documents. A seizure action plan under
Minn. Stat. 121A.24 runs alongside whatever education plan you get, and it is not a substitute
for one. A plan written by the school nurse tells staff what to do for the body. Only a 504 plan
or an IEP makes a support legally enforceable as an education right.

The result screen also gives you the recipient, how to phrase the request, what to do if the
district says no, and a list of what to bring to the meeting. Minnesota evaluation timelines are
set in state rule and they change, so the tool asks the district for its deadline in writing
rather than printing a number.

Full detail is in [Your Child's Rights](../../chapters/07-your-childs-rights/index.md).
