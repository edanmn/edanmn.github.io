# Pharmacy Problem Solver

The pharmacy cannot fill a seizure medicine. Answer a few questions and get the next step for
today, whether the problem is stock, coverage, cost, a national shortage, or distance.

[Open full screen](main.html){ .md-button }

Part of the [Medication Access](../../initiatives/medication-access.md) initiative. The
printable version is the family guide "When the pharmacy can't fill your seizure medicine."

!!! warning
    A learning tool, not medical advice. Never stop or change a seizure medicine without the
    prescriber. If a seizure will not stop, call 911.
