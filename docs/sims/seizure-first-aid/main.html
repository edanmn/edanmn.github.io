When a seizure is a 911 call

  • Convulsive seizure lasting more than 5 minutes
  • A second seizure before the person recovers
  • Trouble breathing, or blue or grey lips
  • Injury, or the seizure happened in water
  • The person's first known seizure
  • They do not wake up afterwards
  • Their seizure action plan says to call

Most seizures stop on their own and do not need an ambulance. Calling when it is not needed costs a family money and does not help the student.

This is practice, not medical advice or certification. A student's care is set by their healthcare provider and their seizure action plan.
